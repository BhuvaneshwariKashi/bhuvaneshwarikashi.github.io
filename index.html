01 / RESEARCH

Research, from spacetime dynamics outward.

I am a Physics PhD student at Florida Atlantic University working in numerical relativity and gravitational-wave astronomy. My research centers on compact-object systems, especially binary neutron-star mergers, and extends to LISA source modeling and detectability, higher-order gravitational-wave modes, spin and precession, and computational methods for relativistic simulations. I am also developing an interest in neutrino behavior in compact-object mergers.
